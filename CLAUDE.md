# Focus Flooring website: rules for Claude

This is the website for **Focus Flooring**, a commercial flooring company in Tampa.
Many different people edit this site, and they may not be technical. Follow every rule below in every session, for every person.

## 1. This is Focus Flooring, not Envision

- Never use Envision's name, brand rules, colors, or voice here.
- **The source of truth is the Focus Flooring Brand Style Guide, Version 1.0 (Draft 1, updated May 2021).** Its rules are written out below, with page numbers. When the guide and the current website disagree, follow the guide. See "Known differences" at the end of this file.
- Only the guide sections copied into this file count. Some pages of the draft guide (History, Values, "What our icon stands for", and others) still contain template placeholder text. Never use or quote them.
- Never make up facts, numbers, client names, quotes, or photos. If something is missing, ask.
- **If a decision is not covered by the guide, stop and ask the person before making it.**

### Voice (guide pages 11 to 12)

- We speak to our customers, our team, and others calmly, with kindness and confidence.
- Our customers and our team members are the reason we're here. The way we speak to them should show how important they are.
- We're genuine, honest, transparent, friendly, and relatable.
- We like to have a good time, but we aren't a particularly witty brand. When we have fun, it's never at the expense of others or in bad taste.
- Marketing and advertising should not feel like selling. If we feel the urge to sell, something is wrong. Our products do that work for us.
- Use contractions. Write like people talk.

### Taglines (guide page 13)

- Taglines may be used in any marketing material, with the logo and brand images.
- Do not combine the brand tagline with campaign-specific taglines or phrases.
- Never rewrite, reword, or edit a tagline in any way.

### Master Style List: writing rules (guide page 14)

When in doubt, the guide says to follow a recent version of the AP Stylebook.

**Headlines**
- Short, clear, and they "hook" the reader into reading more
- Use "and" instead of "&"
- Use sentence case, not Title Case
- Use periods when writing in sentences

**Punctuation**
- Use consistent punctuation
- No spaces around an em dash
- Do not end bulleted or numbered list items with periods, unless the item has more than one sentence
- Do not hyphenate words across lines in paragraphs

**Spelling**
- Use "grey", not "gray"

**Formatting**
- Capitalize the first word in a sentence

**Contact information and times**
- Phone numbers use hyphens, no periods or parentheses: 123-456-7890
- Use standard time format: 1:30PM or 1:30 p.m., not 1330
- The guide also says "Do not use AM or PM in any form." This conflicts with the line above. See "Undecided" below
- Use an en dash for time ranges instead of "through", "to", or "thru"
- Write 12:00 as "noon"
- Never abbreviate days: Monday – Thursday
- In addresses, abbreviate only the state or province. Format (example only, not the real address):
  Focus Flooring
  1234 Main Street
  Tampa, FL 12345

### Logo on the web (guide page 30)

- The logo goes in the upper left-hand corner of the navigation bar.
- Never center the logo on screen, even on small screens.
- The favicon (the 32px by 32px icon next to the web address in the browser) is the only other approved use of the icon in solid form.
- The device icon (shown when someone saves the site to a phone home screen) is 192px by 192px by default.

### Colors (guide pages 33 to 36)

Use only these colors. Any other color is unauthorized. Never change or adjust them.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Focus Slate | `#414042` | Primary |
| Color of Life Green (COL) | `#cce70b` | Primary |
| Color of Life Green Shaded | `#a6b208` | Primary |
| Focus Flooring White | `#f1f1f1` | Use instead of pure white |
| Midnight Black | `#171d1a` | Use instead of pure black |

- Avoid true black (`#000000`) and true white (`#ffffff`). Use Midnight Black and Focus Flooring White instead.
- Use plenty of empty space (white or black) around content.
- Tints (lighter versions of a color) are allowed only when needed, for example a button hover effect. Use 20% steps (80%, 60%, 40%, 20%). Any tint below 60% used as a background needs dark text.
- Never use tints as main colors. Keep them for effects.

### Color contrast (guide pages 37 to 38)

- Everyone should be able to read what we write. Keep a contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1** between text and its background. When unsure, check it (for example at contrast-ratio.com) and tell the person the result.
- **Approved pairings:**
  - White text or icon on Slate
  - White text on COL green
  - White text on Shaded green
  - White text on a light slate background
  - COL green text or icon on Slate
  - Slate text on COL green
  - COL green on Shaded green (subtle; use sparingly)
  - Light or watermark-style uses on white
- **Never:**
  - Slate with dark grey tones (not enough contrast)
  - COL green with low-contrast tints (too close together to read)
  - True black with true white (too much contrast for the brand)
  - COL green on white (contrast too low)
  - Off-brand colors, especially next to brand colors

### Font and typography (guide pages 39 to 46)

- **Font:** Proxima Nova for everything. If it truly can't be used, fall back to Helvetica, then Open Sans. No other fonts.
- **Weights:** Light, Regular, Medium, Bold. Use contrast between heavy and light weights to show what matters most.
- **Website headings (page 43):**
  - Heading 1 (page and major section headings): Proxima Nova Medium, 80px, 50px space below
  - Heading 2 (section headings): Proxima Nova Semi-Bold, 52px, 30px space below
  - Heading 3 (sub headings, call-to-action headings): Proxima Nova Semi-Bold, 34px, 30px space below
  - Heading 4 (minor headings, table labels): Proxima Nova Bold, uppercase, 18px, 20px space below
- **Body text (page 44):** Proxima Nova Light, 18px, 26px line height, grey, 16px space below, max width 800px. Bold text uses Proxima Nova Medium.
- **Block quotes:** 30px, 36px line height, 3px line on the left, 40px space on the left.
- **Text links:** Proxima Nova Medium, green, underlined.
- **Lists (page 45):** Proxima Nova Light, 18px, 26px line height, grey, 20px indent.
- **Six type rules (page 42):**
  1. Align text left with a ragged right edge
  2. For contrast, skip a weight and double the size between two text elements
  3. Line up baselines or x-heights when text sits side by side
  4. Watch the ragged edge; avoid one-word last lines
  5. Give things space, but keep related items together
  6. Keep lines between 45 and 70 characters long
- **Never (page 46):**
  - Unapproved fonts
  - Stretched or squished text
  - Outlines or drop shadows on text
  - Text at any angle other than 0° or 90° (and 90° text reads upward)
  - Centered or fully justified text that runs more than one line. The guide says there are no exceptions.

## 2. Explain things simply

- Use plain, everyday words. No jargon. If a technical word is needed, explain it in one short sentence.
- Go one step at a time. Say what you are about to do, do it, then say what happened.
- Lead with the answer. Keep messages short.
- When you need a decision, ask one clear question.

## 3. One change at a time

- Make only one change per request (for example, one headline or one photo).
- If a request contains several changes, list them and do them one by one, with approval between each.
- Do not "tidy up" or fix other things along the way. If you notice a problem, mention it and ask.

## How every edit works

1. The person tells you the edit.
2. You make it in the private preview and show before-and-after screenshots they can share with leadership.
3. Nothing goes live until the person says **"go"**. When they say "go", publish that one change.

## 4. Always show before-and-after screenshots

- Screenshots should be clean and easy to share with leadership: the affected part of the page, at normal size, with no tools or code showing.
- Before changing anything, start the private preview and take a screenshot of the affected page ("before").
- After the change, take a screenshot of the same spot ("after").
- Show both side by side and say in one sentence what changed.
- Include a phone-size screenshot too when the change affects layout or long text.
- How to preview: run `pnpm install`, then `npx nuxt dev --port 3000`, then screenshot `http://localhost:3000` with Playwright. Do not download a new browser; one is already installed.

## 5. Never publish until the person says "go"

- Do not save (commit), upload (push), or publish anything until the person types **"go"** for that specific change.
- Approval for one change does not cover the next one.
- Any update to the `main` branch publishes the live site automatically through Vercel. Never update `main` without a "go".
- When the person says "go", publish that one change to `main`: save only what was approved and nothing else, then confirm what went live and whether Vercel shows a green check or a red X.

## 6. Keep every change small and easy to undo

- Change as few lines as possible.
- One change = one save (commit), with a plain-English description such as "Change About Us headline".
- Before saving, show the exact words that changed (old and new).
- If the person wants to undo, revert that one save. Never rewrite or delete history.

## 7. Where content lives

- **Page text** is written directly in the page files in `app/pages/` and the sections in `app/components/`.
  - Example: the home page headline is in `app/components/HomeHero.vue`; the About Us core values are in `app/pages/about-us.vue`.
- **Page photos** are in `public/`. New photos go there; resize them first so each file is small (2 MB or less).
- **Google search titles and descriptions** are set at the top of each page file (`useSeoMeta`) and in `nuxt.config.ts`.
- **Portfolio projects and team members come from Airtable, not the code.**
  - Projects show on the site when their Airtable row is marked "live". Project photos come from ImageKit.
  - To add, remove, or edit a project or team member, the person should update Airtable. Do not try to change these in the code.
  - The private preview cannot reach Airtable, so the Portfolio and Meet the Team pages look empty there. That is expected; say so when showing screenshots of those pages.

## Known differences between the guide and the live site

The live site does not match the guide yet. **New edits follow the guide.** Do not change the site to fix these unless the person asks for that specific change.

- **Font:** the guide says Proxima Nova. The site uses Inter.
- **Headlines:** the guide says sentence case. The site uses all-caps headlines (for example "ELIMINATING YOUR RISK THROUGH OUR PROCESS.").

## Undecided

These questions are open. Do not ask about them again. Do not make choices that depend on them; if an edit runs into one, tell the person it's marked undecided here and leave that part as it is.

1. **Times:** the guide says to write "1:30PM or 1:30 p.m." but also says "Do not use AM or PM in any form."
2. **Tagline:** guide page 13 shows "Deep expertise coupled with sincere empathy is our recipe for successful outcomes." It's not confirmed whether this is the real tagline or placeholder text. Don't use it.
3. **Link color:** the guide says text links are green, but also bans green text on white (contrast too low).
4. **More differences from the live site:** the site's off-white (`#eceded`) and light yellow (`#e1f555`) are not guide colors, and the home page headline is centered across several lines, which the guide bans. Not yet listed as known differences.
5. **Email signatures:** the guide's "Email Signatures..." line is unfinished.
6. **Proxima Nova licensing:** the guide calls Proxima Nova free, but it is generally a paid font (available with an Adobe Fonts subscription). Confirm Focus can use it on the website before switching fonts.
